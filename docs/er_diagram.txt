KEY

  ──►   one-to-many (parent has many child rows)
  ─0►   optional (nullable FK / not every parent row produces a child)
  [self]  self-referencing FK on the same table

ACTIVITIES (fans in from every stage of the funnel — not drawn above to
keep the main diagram legible; each activity row carries whichever of
these five FKs its own context involves, and may carry none of them for
a general internal task):

  contacts ───────────┤

  opportunities ───────┤                          -- every activity has an
  customers ──────────┘                          owning/performing rep)

FUNNEL GATE (enforced by generation + validation, not just by FK type):

  lead (Sales Qualified (SQL)) ──► opportunity ──(Closed Won only)──► customer ──► transactions / support cases

A lead that never reaches Sales Qualified never gets an opportunity_id
pointing to it; an opportunity that is Closed Lost or still open never
gets a customer_id pointing to it. This is the single most important
business rule in the schema — see business_rules.md.
