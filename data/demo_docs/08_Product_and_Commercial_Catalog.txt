Bundling rules

PulseCRM Enterprise + InsightIQ: common combination when an account needs both pipeline management and external business intelligence.

OmniDesk + RouteVision: suitable for service-heavy field operations.

SecureSync + DataBridge: suitable when secure integration and data quality are both explicit requirements.

InsightIQ can be paired with any core CRM deployment when account intelligence is required.
